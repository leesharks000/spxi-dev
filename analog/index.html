Handwriting, and what a machine can do with it

Three papers on making a handwritten artifact machine-eligible without dissolving it into text. A specification, a field survey of the literatures around it, and an operations paper on what the position is worth.

Machine-Eligible Handwritten Artifacts

v1.0 final form · edition of record 2026-07-21 · deposit #1409

What it takes for a photograph of a handwritten page to enter machine reception as evidence rather than as a transcription — capture, declaration, enrollment, and the distinction between origination and composition.

The Photograph of the Handwritten Original

A discipline-by-discipline treatment of the adjacent literatures — papyrology and machine reading, handwritten text recognition, writer identification, archival authenticity, content provenance, persistent identifiers — each stated with what it establishes, what it assumes, and what it cannot do.

The Analog Advance

Taking the handwritten niche: the enrollment path, the eight deliverables, and what the position costs to hold. The specification says how; the survey says why; this says when, where, and in what order.
